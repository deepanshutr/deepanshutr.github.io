AssetMantle is a framework that lets creators and brands run their own marketplaces for non-fungible tokens (NFTs - unique digital assets recorded on a blockchain). Its mainnet went live in April 2022, built with Cosmos tooling so its assets can move between chains.

    
Deepanshu founded AssetMantle and leads product and growth. Earlier he led development of interNFT, the shared standard that lets NFTs work across blockchains, and was a key contributor to pSTAKE. Code lives at github.com/AssetMantle, product at assetmantle.one.
